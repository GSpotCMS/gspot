# Welcome to GSpot CMS

**Find the Spot. Hit it. Ship it.**

A flat-file, MCP-native content management system built for builders who got tired of waiting for permission.

## What is GSpot CMS?

- **Flat-file core** — No database. No migrations. No drama.
- **MCP-native tooling** — AI tools built into the core, not bolted on.
- **Blade templating** — Clean, expressive templates.
- **Plugin architecture** — Extend everything, break nothing.
- **Vibe presets** — Swap the entire aesthetic in one click.
